## What Affects Commercial Solar Installation Cost?

### 1. System size and design

Larger projects can benefit from economies of scale, although the final cost depends on the system layout, electrical design, and site requirements.

### 2. Solar modules

Module technology, output rating, manufacturer, product warranties, and performance specifications affect the equipment budget.

### 3. Inverters and monitoring

The choice of inverter architecture, monitoring features, protection equipment, and service arrangements can influence both initial cost and ongoing maintenance.

### 4. Rooftop type and structural requirements

RCC roofs and metal sheds require different mounting solutions. Structural assessments, roof condition, waterproofing, and wind-load requirements may add to the project scope.

### 5. Electrical infrastructure

Cables, switchgear, protection systems, earthing, transformer capacity, and electrical upgrades can affect the total installation cost.

### 6. Grid connection and approvals

Metering, utility coordination, interconnection requirements, and applicable local procedures should be confirmed before project execution.

### 7. Site access and installation logistics

Roof access, lifting equipment, working conditions, safety arrangements, and the distance between equipment and connection points may affect labour and logistics costs.

### 8. EPC contract scope

A turnkey EPC quotation may include design, supply, installation, testing, and commissioning. A supply-only quote may exclude several of these items, so compare quotations on the same scope.

## Commercial Solar ROI and Payback

Consider this illustrative example for a 100 kW commercial rooftop solar installation.

| Metric | Illustrative Assumption |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed capacity | 100 kW |
| Estimated project cost | ₹38–48 lakh |
| Assumed annual generation | 150,000 units |
| Assumed electricity tariff | ₹9 per unit |
| Illustrative annual electricity value | ₹13.5 lakh |

The estimated electricity value is calculated as 150,000 units × ₹9 per unit. It assumes that all generated electricity offsets electricity purchases at the stated tariff. Actual savings can be lower or higher depending on solar generation, daytime consumption, system losses, export compensation, tariff structure, and applicable charges.

A simple payback estimate should use the actual project price and realistic annual savings. For a more complete financial assessment, also consider operating and maintenance costs, financing costs, tariff changes, equipment performance, and any applicable tax treatment or incentives. Confirm eligibility and current rules with qualified advisers before including incentives in a business case.

## How to Compare Commercial Solar Quotations

Before selecting an installer, review each proposal against the same checklist:

- Confirm the installed capacity and proposed system design.
- Compare solar module and inverter models, specifications, and warranties.
- Check mounting structures, roof protection, and structural requirements.
- Verify cabling, earthing, switchgear, and electrical safety provisions.
- Identify exclusions, civil work, and possible infrastructure upgrades.
- Confirm who is responsible for utility coordination and approvals.
- Review testing, commissioning, monitoring, and operation and maintenance coverage.
- Ask for the assumptions behind projected generation and savings.
- Compare the complete project price and contract scope—not only the price per kW.
- Request a site-specific proposal before making a final investment decision.
